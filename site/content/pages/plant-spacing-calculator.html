How to Use the Plant Spacing Calculator

  1. Select Units (ft/in or m/cm).
  2. Pick a Crop Preset or choose Custom spacing.
  3. Enter Bed Length and Bed Width.
  4. Adjust In-row and Row spacing as needed.
  5. Click Calculate → see rows, plants/row, total plants and a printable grid plan.

Plant Spacing FAQ

Are the presets universal?

Presets are proven starting points for intensive beds. Always adapt to variety vigor and climate.

How do I use the printable plan?

Click Print Plan to produce an SVG grid. Tape it to your bed plan or save as PDF for the project file.

Square-foot gardening?

Set in-row = row spacing (e.g., 12 in) to generate a square grid; the planner will match the squares.

Companion planting?

Run the calculator per crop, then overlay in your plan: e.g., basil between tomato rows at 30×30 cm.

Why do my totals drop when I increase spacing?

Larger spacing reduces plants per row/rows—use it for bigger fruit size, airflow, and disease control.
