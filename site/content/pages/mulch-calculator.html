Mulch Calculator FAQ

What depth should I use?

General beds: 2–3 in (5–7.5 cm). Weed suppression: up to 4 in. Keep mulch 2–3 in away from stems/trunks.

Which bag size should I select?

Common retail sizes: 2 cu ft, 3 cu ft, and 50 L (~1.77 cu ft). Use “Custom” if your local bag differs.

How accurate is the result?

Regular shapes are highly accurate. For curved/irregular beds, over-estimate slightly for edges and settling.

What’s better: bags or bulk?

Bags are convenient for small areas; bulk (yd³) is cheaper for large projects. Use the cost fields to compare.

Does mulch type change volume?

Volume stays the same; weight can vary with wood type and moisture. The calculator outputs volume + bags.
