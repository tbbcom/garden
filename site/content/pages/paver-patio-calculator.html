Paver & Patio FAQ

How much waste should I use?

5–10% for straight patterns; 10–15% for herringbone/diagonals/complex borders.

What joint width is typical?

Polymeric sand joints commonly 3–6 mm (⅛–¼ in). Follow the paver manufacturer.

How deep should the base be?

Walkways: 3–4 in gravel + 1 in sand; driveways: 6–8 in gravel + 1 in sand (soil/drainage dependent).

How do I estimate edging?

The tool outputs perimeter length—order edge restraints + spikes accordingly.

Does pattern change the count?

Pattern affects waste more than net count; set a higher waste % for complex patterns.
