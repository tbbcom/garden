Fertilizer FAQ

What target N should I use?

Typical single application: 0.5–1.0 lb N/1,000 ft² (0.25–0.5 kg N/100 m²). Follow label & local guidelines.

What do the P and K numbers mean?

N = Nitrogen, P = Phosphate (P₂O₅), K = Potash (K₂O), by weight %. The tool reports applied totals.

How many applications per year?

Commonly 2–4, depending on grass type and climate. Split into smaller doses for safety.

Liquid vs granular?

Granular is common for lawns; liquids are fast-acting. Always follow the product label rate.

Can I over-fertilize?

Yes—risk of burn and runoff. Never exceed label rates. Water-in if required.
