Irrigation FAQ

What efficiency should I use?

Typical: Drip 85–95%, Sprinklers 60–80% depending on wind/overlap. Start at 80% if unsure.

What is precipitation rate?

How fast your system applies water (in/hr or mm/hr). Manufacturers publish nozzle charts; you can also catch-can test.

How deep should I water?

Turf: 0.5–1.0 in per cycle; vegetables: 0.5–1.0 in; trees: fewer, deeper soaks. Adjust by soil/drainage.

Why are my results different from the controller?

Controller programs are time-based; this tool aligns run-time to actual water applied so you can tune programs.

How often should I water?

Use local weather/ET and plant needs. Deep & infrequent for established turf; frequent & light for seedlings.
