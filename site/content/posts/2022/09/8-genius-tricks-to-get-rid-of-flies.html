Quick answer

To get rid of flies, remove what they breed in and feed on first: take out the trash, clean up pet waste, and clear rotting fruit and dirty drains. Then catch the adults with traps (a vinegar trap for fruit flies, sticky or light traps for house flies) and keep new ones out with screens and sealed gaps. Traps alone won't solve the problem while flies are still breeding nearby.

A few flies usually means there is something nearby they are feeding or laying eggs in. House flies breed in garbage and animal waste, fruit flies in overripe produce, and small drain flies in the slimy film inside drains. Identify which fly you have, remove the source, and the rest of the methods below will clear the house quickly.

Key takeaways
  • Find the source: trash, pet waste, rotting fruit or a dirty drain.
  • Match the trap to the fly: vinegar for fruit flies, sticky or light traps for house flies.
  • Block entry: repair window screens and seal gaps around doors.
  • Use airflow: a fan over a patio table keeps flies from landing.

How do you get rid of flies in the house? 8 methods that work

  1. Remove breeding sites. Empty indoor bins daily, use bins with tight lids, rinse recycling, and pick up pet waste promptly. This is the single most effective step.
  2. Clean up food. Refrigerate ripe fruit, wipe up sticky spills, and don't leave dirty dishes or pet food out.
  3. Clean drains. Scrub drains with a stiff brush and hot soapy water to remove the film drain flies breed in.
  4. Set a vinegar trap for fruit flies. Fill a small jar with apple cider vinegar and add a drop of dish soap so the flies sink. Cover with plastic wrap poked with a few small holes, and replace every few days.
  5. Use sticky or light traps for house flies. Hang sticky fly ribbons out of the way of people and pets, or use an enclosed UV light trap away from windows so it doesn't draw flies in from outside.
  6. Swat strategically. A swatter or electric racket is fine for the last few stragglers once the source is gone.
  7. Keep flies out. Repair torn window and door screens, add door sweeps, and seal gaps around pipes and vents.
  8. Use moving air. Flies avoid steady breezes, so a fan in the kitchen or on the patio makes it hard for them to land.

How do you keep flies away outside?

  • Pick up dog waste in the yard at least every few days.
  • Keep outdoor bins closed and rinse them out regularly.
  • Bury food scraps in the center of the compost pile and keep it covered. See how to compost kitchen scraps.
  • Use a fan over outdoor dining tables and cover food.

Do natural fly repellents work?

Strong scents such as peppermint, lavender or eucalyptus may make flies less likely to linger, but the effect is mild and short-lived. They are no substitute for removing breeding sites and using traps. Keep essential oils away from pets, especially cats, and never apply undiluted oils to skin.

Are house flies harmful?

House flies feed and breed on waste, so they can carry bacteria such as Salmonella and E. coli onto food and surfaces. Keep food covered, wash produce, and clean surfaces where flies have landed. If flies keep appearing in large numbers indoors, a hidden source such as a dead animal in a wall may be the cause; a pest control professional can find it.

Frequently asked questions

Why are there so many flies in my house all of a sudden?

A sudden swarm usually means flies hatched from a nearby source: garbage, a forgotten bag of produce, pet waste, a drain, or a dead animal in a wall or attic. Find and remove it, and the numbers drop within days.

What is the best homemade fly trap?

For fruit flies, a jar of apple cider vinegar with a drop of dish soap, covered with plastic wrap poked with small holes. House flies are better caught with sticky ribbons or enclosed light traps.

Does a bag of water keep flies away?

There's little evidence that hanging bags of water repel flies. Removing breeding sites and using fans and screens works far better.

How long do house flies live?

Usually about 2–4 weeks, but a female can lay hundreds of eggs, which is why removing breeding sites matters more than killing individual flies.

The bottom line

Get rid of flies by removing what they breed in, then trapping the adults and keeping new ones out with screens. Match the trap to the fly, clean drains and bins, and use a fan where you eat outdoors.
