Quick answer

Grow a curry leaf plant (Murraya koenigii) in a pot of well-drained potting mix in full sun to light shade, water when the top inch (2.5 cm) of soil is dry, and feed lightly during the growing season. It's frost-tender: in the US it can live outdoors year-round only in about zones 9b–11, and in the UK and colder US areas it should spend winter indoors in a bright spot above 50°F (10°C).

Fresh curry leaves give South Indian and Sri Lankan dishes their aroma, and dried leaves lose much of it, which is why many cooks grow their own. The curry leaf plant is a small tropical tree in the citrus family. It is not related to curry powder or to the silvery "curry plant" (Helichrysum italicum), which smells similar but is not used for cooking in the same way.

Key takeaways
  • Right plant: Murraya koenigii (also listed as Bergera koenigii).
  • Start with a nursery plant for the quickest results; seeds must be very fresh.
  • Warmth and light: 6+ hours of sun; protect from frost.
  • Water and drainage: moist, never soggy; reduce water in winter.
  • Harvest often by snipping whole stems to keep the plant bushy.

How do you start a curry leaf plant?

  • Nursery plant (easiest): buy a young plant from an Indian grocery, nursery or online seller; you can harvest within months.
  • Seed: use fresh seeds from ripe black berries, remove the pulp, and sow ¼ inch (6 mm) deep in moist seed mix at 70–85°F (21–29°C). Old, dried seeds rarely germinate. Expect sprouts in 3–6 weeks.
  • Suckers or root cuttings: established plants often send up suckers that can be dug up with some root and potted.

How do you plant and pot curry leaf?

  1. Use a pot with drainage holes, about 2 inches (5 cm) wider than the root ball; move up a size every year or two.
  2. Fill with a free-draining potting mix with some added perlite or grit.
  3. Plant at the same depth it grew before and water well.
  4. Place it in the sunniest spot you have: a south-facing window indoors, or full sun to light shade outdoors in summer.

How do you care for a curry leaf plant?

  • Water: when the top inch (2.5 cm) of soil is dry in summer; much less in winter, when growth slows.
  • Feed: a balanced liquid fertilizer every 2–4 weeks in spring and summer. Yellowing young leaves can point to iron or magnesium shortage; a citrus fertilizer helps.
  • Temperature: happiest at 65–85°F (18–29°C). Bring it indoors before nights fall below about 50°F (10°C).
  • Winter: some leaf drop indoors in winter is normal. Keep it away from cold drafts and heaters, and water sparingly.
  • Pests: check for scale, aphids and mealybugs, especially indoors; wipe them off or use insecticidal soap following the label.
  • Flowers: pinching off flower clusters keeps the plant's energy in the leaves.

Can you grow curry leaf outdoors in the US or UK?

In the US, curry leaf can grow outdoors all year in roughly USDA zones 9b–11 (parts of Florida, south Texas, Southern California and Hawaii). Find your zone by ZIP code. In colder zones and in the UK, keep it in a container outdoors in summer and indoors from autumn to spring.

How do you harvest curry leaves?

Once the plant has several branches, snip whole leaf stems (each holds many small leaflets) rather than pulling off individual leaflets. Cutting just above a leaf node encourages branching and a bushier plant. Use leaves fresh, or freeze them on the stem in a bag; freezing keeps more aroma than drying.

Frequently asked questions

Why are my curry leaf plant's leaves turning yellow?

Usually overwatering, cold, or a nutrient shortage. Let the soil dry slightly between waterings, keep it warm, and feed with a citrus or balanced fertilizer in the growing season.

Can you grow curry leaf from store-bought leaves?

No. Leaves won't root. Start from fresh seeds, a sucker or a nursery plant.

How fast does a curry leaf plant grow?

Slowly in the first year, then faster once established. A healthy potted plant can be harvested lightly within its first year.

Is the curry plant the same as curry leaf?

No. The silvery "curry plant" (Helichrysum italicum) only smells similar. Curry leaf is Murraya koenigii.

The bottom line

Grow curry leaf in a sunny spot in well-drained soil, water when the top inch is dry, feed through summer, and keep it warm in winter. Harvest whole stems often for a bushy plant and a steady supply of fresh leaves.
