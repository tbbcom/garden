Quick answer

Multiply the area in square feet by the depth in inches and divide by 324 to get cubic yards. A 1,000 sq ft lawn area 4 inches deep needs about 12.3 cubic yards (9.4 m³). A 100 sq ft bed 6 inches deep needs about 1.85 cubic yards. Add about 10–20% if the soil is loose and will settle.

Topsoil is sold by the cubic yard in bulk, or in bags of 0.75 to 1 cubic foot (or litres in the UK). Order too little and you'll pay delivery twice. Use the topsoil calculator for exact figures, or the topsoil & gravel calculator for weight and cost.

Key takeaways
  • Formula: area (sq ft) × depth (in) ÷ 324 = cubic yards.
  • Metric: area (m²) × depth (m) = m³ (100 mm over 10 m² = 1 m³).
  • New lawn: 4–6 in (100–150 mm). Garden beds: 6–12 in. Top dressing: ¼–½ in.
  • Weight: about 1–1.3 tons per cubic yard, more when wet.
  • Bags: 27 one-cubic-foot bags or 36 bags of 0.75 cu ft make one cubic yard.

How do you calculate how much topsoil you need?

  1. Measure the area in square feet. For lawns, see how to measure lawn square footage.
  2. Choose the depth in inches for the job (table below).
  3. Multiply area × depth, then divide by 324 (324 is the number of square feet that 1 cubic yard covers at 1 inch deep).
  4. Add 10–20% for settling if you're filling, building up a bed or leveling low spots.

Example: a 20 × 30 ft area (600 sq ft) for a new lawn at 4 in: 600 × 4 ÷ 324 = 7.4 cu yd. With 15% for settling, order about 8.5 cubic yards.

How many bags of topsoil do you need?

Divide the cubic feet you need by the bag size. A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, so it takes 27 one-cubic-foot bags, 36 bags of 0.75 cu ft or about 18 bags of 1.5 cu ft. In the UK, 1 m³ is 1,000 litres, or about 25 bags of 40 L. Bags are handy for under about a cubic yard. Above that, bulk delivery is usually far cheaper and saves a lot of plastic.

Topsoil, garden soil or compost: which one?

Screened topsoil is the right base for lawns and leveling. For vegetable beds, blend topsoil with 25–50% compost, or buy a premixed garden soil. See how to build a raised garden bed for a soil mix and the raised bed soil calculator for volumes. Ask the supplier whether the soil has been screened and is weed-free, and look at a sample before a large order, because cheap fill dirt is often sold as topsoil.

Frequently asked questions

How much topsoil do I need for 1,000 square feet?

About 12.3 cubic yards at 4 inches deep and 18.5 cubic yards at 6 inches; about 1.5 cubic yards to top-dress at ½ inch.

How many square feet does a yard of topsoil cover?

About 324 sq ft at 1 inch deep, 108 sq ft at 3 inches, 81 sq ft at 4 inches and 54 sq ft at 6 inches.

How many bags of topsoil make a yard?

27 bags of 1 cubic foot, or 36 bags of 0.75 cubic feet.

How much does a yard of topsoil weigh?

Roughly 1 to 1.3 tons (2,000–2,600 lb) depending on moisture and how much clay or organic matter it contains.

The bottom line

Topsoil needed in cubic yards = square feet × depth in inches ÷ 324. Use 4–6 inches for a new lawn, 8–12 inches for beds and no more than ½ inch for top dressing, and add 10–20% when the soil will settle.
