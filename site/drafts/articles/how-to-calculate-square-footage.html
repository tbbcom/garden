Quick answer

For a rectangle, multiply length × width in feet (a 12 × 15 ft room is 180 sq ft). For an irregular space, split it into rectangles, triangles (½ × base × height) and circles (3.14 × radius²), calculate each, and add them. To convert to square metres, multiply square feet by 0.0929.

Square footage is the starting number for almost every home project: flooring, paint, tile, carpet, insulation, heating and cooling, even pricing a house. You only need a tape measure, a notepad and a little multiplication. The square footage calculator adds up mixed shapes for you.

Key takeaways
  • Rectangle: length × width.
  • Triangle: ½ × base × height.
  • Circle: 3.1416 × radius².
  • Irregular rooms: split into simple shapes and add them up.
  • Convert: sq ft × 0.0929 = m²; m² × 10.764 = sq ft.

How do you calculate the square footage of a room?

  1. Measure the length of the room wall to wall, in feet.
  2. Measure the width.
  3. Multiply length × width.

If a measurement includes inches, convert them to decimal feet by dividing by 12: 12 ft 6 in = 12.5 ft. A room 12 ft 6 in × 10 ft is 125 sq ft.

How do you calculate square footage of an irregular room?

  1. Sketch the room and divide it into rectangles, triangles and circles or half-circles.
  2. Measure and calculate each shape separately.
  3. Add the areas together; subtract any cut-outs you won't cover.

Example (L-shaped room): a 14 × 10 ft section (140 sq ft) plus an 8 × 6 ft alcove (48 sq ft) = 188 sq ft.

How do you calculate the square footage of a house?

Add up the finished, heated living areas room by room, or measure the exterior walls of each finished floor and multiply. In the US, the ANSI Z765 standard used by many appraisers measures finished area from the exterior walls and excludes garages, unfinished basements and spaces with low ceilings. Listing and appraisal rules vary, so for a sale or valuation, follow your local standard. In the UK, floor area on property listings is usually given as the internal area in m².

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate square feet from inches?

Multiply length × width in inches, then divide by 144. For example, 60 × 48 in = 2,880 ÷ 144 = 20 sq ft.

How many square feet are in a 12×12 room?

144 square feet (about 13.4 m²).

How do I convert square feet to square metres?

Multiply by 0.0929. 1,000 sq ft is about 92.9 m².

Do closets count in a room's square footage?

For flooring, include any closet you'll floor. For home listings, rules vary; most include finished closets in total living area.

The bottom line

Square footage = length × width for rectangles; split anything irregular into simple shapes and add them. Multiply by 0.0929 for square metres, and add waste for flooring and tile.
