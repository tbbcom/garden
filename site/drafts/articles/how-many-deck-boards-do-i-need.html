Boards, Joists and Screws ---
Quick answer

Divide the deck's width (measured across the joists) by the board width plus the gap, about 5.625 inches (143 mm) for standard 5.5-inch boards, to get the number of rows. Multiply by the number of boards per row, then add 10% for waste (15% for diagonal layouts). A 12 × 16 ft deck with boards running the 16 ft length needs 26 rows of 16 ft boards, or about 29 boards with waste.

Deck boards are usually sold in 8, 10, 12, 16 and 20 ft lengths (about 2.4–6 m). Choosing lengths that match your deck reduces joints and waste, and the right gap keeps water draining and boards from buckling. The deck calculator works out boards, joists and screws for any size.

Key takeaways
  • Board width: "6-inch" deck boards are actually about 5.5 in (140 mm) wide.
  • Gap: about ⅛–¼ in (3–6 mm) between boards; follow the manufacturer for composite.
  • Waste: 10% straight, 15% diagonal or picture-frame layouts.
  • Joists: usually 16 in (400 mm) on center for wood; often 12 in for composite or diagonal decking.
  • Screws: two per board at every joist.

How do you calculate deck boards?

  1. Measure the deck width in the direction across the boards, in inches.
  2. Divide by board width + gap (5.5 + 0.125 = 5.625 in for standard boards) to get rows. Round up.
  3. Work out boards per row: deck length ÷ board length, rounded up. Pick a board length that divides evenly if you can.
  4. Multiply rows × boards per row.
  5. Add 10–15% for cuts, defects and mistakes.

Example: 12 × 16 ft deck, boards running the 16 ft way: 144 in ÷ 5.625 = 25.6, so 26 rows. With 16 ft boards, that's 26 boards, or 29 with 10% waste.

How far apart should deck joists be?

Most wood decking is installed on joists 16 inches (400 mm) on center. Many composite boards and all diagonal layouts call for 12 inches (300 mm). Thinner boards need closer joists. Always follow the decking manufacturer's span rating; the deck joist span calculator helps you size the joists themselves.

How many screws do you need for decking?

Use two screws per board at every joist. For a 12 × 16 ft deck with joists 16 in on center running the 12 ft way, that's 13 joists × 26 rows × 2 = 676 screws, so buy a box of about 750. Use exterior-rated or stainless screws, or hidden fasteners recommended for your board.

Frequently asked questions

How many deck boards do I need for a 12×12 deck?

About 26 rows of 12 ft boards, or roughly 29 boards with 10% waste, using standard 5.5-inch boards.

What gap should I leave between deck boards?

About ⅛–¼ inch (3–6 mm) for most boards. Wet pressure-treated boards can be butted tight because they shrink as they dry. Follow the manufacturer for composite.

Should deck boards run parallel or perpendicular to the house?

Usually parallel to the house, perpendicular to the joists. They can also run diagonally, which needs closer joists and more waste.

Do I need a permit for a deck?

Often yes, especially for decks attached to the house or raised above a set height. Check with your local building department.

The bottom line

Rows = deck width ÷ 5.625 in; boards = rows × boards per row; then add 10–15%. Match board lengths to the deck, space joists to the manufacturer's rating, and use two screws per board at each joist.
