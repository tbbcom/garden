Quick answer

Board feet = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12. One board foot is a piece 1 in thick, 12 in wide and 1 ft long, which is 144 cubic inches (about 2.36 litres). A 2 × 4 that is 8 ft long is 2 × 4 × 8 ÷ 12 = 5.33 board feet. If all three measurements are in inches, divide by 144 instead.

Hardwood is priced by the board foot, and so are many log sales and big softwood orders. Knowing the formula lets you compare prices and buy the right amount. The board foot calculator handles lists of boards, and the log board foot calculator estimates yield from logs.

Key takeaways
  • Formula: T (in) × W (in) × L (ft) ÷ 12, or T × W × L (all in inches) ÷ 144.
  • Softwood is calculated on nominal size (a 2×4 counts as 2 × 4 in).
  • Hardwood uses rough thickness in quarters: 4/4 = 1 in, 6/4 = 1.5 in, 8/4 = 2 in.
  • Metric: 1 board foot = 0.00236 m³; 1 m³ = about 424 board feet.
  • Waste: buy 15–30% extra for hardwood projects (defects, grain matching and offcuts).

How do you calculate board feet?

  1. Measure the thickness and width in inches and the length in feet.
  2. Multiply the three numbers.
  3. Divide by 12.
  4. Multiply by the number of boards, then by the price per board foot for cost.

Example: 6 boards of 4/4 cherry, 7 in wide and 8 ft long: 1 × 7 × 8 ÷ 12 = 4.67 BF each × 6 = 28 BF. At $9 per BF, that's $252.

Nominal, actual and rough sizes: which do you use?

Softwood dimensional lumber (2×4s, 2×6s) is figured on nominal size even though a 2×4 actually measures 1½ × 3½ in. Hardwood is sold rough-sawn by quarter thickness, so a 4/4 board counts as 1 in thick even after planing to about ¹³⁄₁₆ in. Width is measured on the actual board, because hardwood comes in random widths. For surfaced (S2S or S4S) hardwood, ask whether the price is per rough or surfaced board foot. Most yards use rough.

How do you convert board feet to metric?

1 board foot = 144 in³ = 2,360 cm³ = 0.00236 m³, so m³ × 423.8 = board feet. In the UK and Europe, timber is usually sold by the linear metre or the cubic metre, so to compare prices convert the m³ price ÷ 424 to get a price per board foot.

Frequently asked questions

How many board feet is a 2x4x8?

5.33 board feet (2 × 4 × 8 ÷ 12), using nominal dimensions.

What is a board foot?

A volume of wood 1 inch thick, 12 inches wide and 1 foot long: 144 cubic inches or about 2.36 litres.

Do you use actual or nominal size for board feet?

Nominal size for softwood dimensional lumber, and rough thickness (4/4, 8/4 and so on) with actual width for hardwood.

How many board feet are in a cubic metre?

About 424 board feet.

The bottom line

Board feet = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12. Use nominal sizes for softwood, quarter thickness for hardwood, and buy 15–30% extra for furniture-grade projects.
