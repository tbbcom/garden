## What makes a tool beat what's ranking (apply to every build)
The current #1s are thin: one shape, one unit, no explanation. Information gain is what Google's helpful-content systems and AI Overviews reward. Each tool ships with:

1. **Answer above the inputs**: one sentence with the key number for the head query (e.g. "1 cubic yard of gravel ≈ 1.4 tons and covers 108 sq ft at 3 in deep"). This is the line Google's AI Overview and ChatGPT quote: **zero-click AI citation + featured snippet** target.
2. **Every unit a buyer orders in**: cu ft, cu yd, tons, bags (0.5 / 2 / 3 cu ft or 40/50/60/80 lb), pallets, truckloads, plus cost with an editable price. Competitors usually give one.
3. **Multiple areas and shapes** summed in one result (rectangle, circle, triangle, L-shape).
4. **"Show the math"** panel with the formula and the user's numbers substituted in. AI answer engines cite formulas, and it is an E-E-A-T trust signal.
5. **Live SVG diagram** that redraws as inputs change (stairs, retaining wall, sod layout).
6. **State in the URL** (`?l=20&w=10&d=3`) so results can be shared and bookmarked. This brings repeat visits and natural links.
7. **Printable shopping list** with an email-me option for the list (feeds the email list in GROWTH-PLAN §3).
8. **Embed code** (as already done for frost dates) with a credit link: the cheapest white-hat link building available.
9. **Sources box** citing the published density/coverage figures used (manufacturer spec sheets, NCMA for block, extension services for seed and sod rates), plus "Last reviewed" date and author. This is the E-E-A-T layer.
10. **Schema**: `WebApplication` (free offer, no ratings unless they are real), `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage` markup (no longer earns a rich result for this site type, but it gives AI parsers clean Q&A), and `Dataset` on the zone and frost data pages (eligible for Google Dataset Search). **Never** add `aggregateRating` without real reviews; that is the likely cause of the 2026 ranking collapse (see SEO-AUDIT.md).

## Where to host them
**Recommendation: build new calculators on `tools.homegardenlot.com`** (the GitHub Pages site already built by `garden-calc/scripts/build-site.mjs`), not as Blogger pages.
- Blogger pages carry the theme's weight and the 1.4 s apex→www redirect. The tools site loads only what the tool needs, so it passes Core Web Vitals easily (a confirmed, if light, page-experience signal).
- Programmatic pages (50 state zone pages; gravel by stone type; sod by grass type) are impossible to maintain by hand in Blogger and take one template on the tools site.
- Cost: $0 (GitHub Pages + Actions).
- Trade-off: the subdomain builds authority partly separately from www. Offset it by linking every tool from the relevant Blogger posts (the 60-post content plan already maps each post to a tool) and from the main nav.
- Keep the existing Blogger mulch and planting-calendar URLs where they are. Moving them would reset their history.

## Revenue mechanism and timeframe
- **Traffic**: if the top 7 reach their target positions, Ubersuggest's position-click estimates add up to roughly **35–55k visits/month at the spring–summer peak**. Expect 3–6 months from launch to those positions, which means mid-2027 for most of the list.
- **AdSense**: US home-improvement traffic usually earns well above the average for general content. Calculator pages hold visitors longer (inputs, re-calculations), which adds ad impressions per visit.
- **Amazon Associates** on the result panel ("You need 14 bags; here's what we'd use"): someone who just calculated a quantity has purchase intent, which is the highest-converting placement on the site. Disclose it next to the links.
- **Links**: the zone pages and embeds are the link magnets that lift DA, and higher DA then pulls up every other tool. This is the compounding part.
