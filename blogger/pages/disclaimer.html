Safety, chemicals and DIY

  • Pesticides and fertilizers: the product label is the law. Follow it for rates, protective equipment, timing and pets and children. We don't give dosage advice beyond the label.
  • Digging: in the U.S., call 811 a few days before you dig to have buried utility lines marked.
  • Electrical, gas, structural and tree work: hire a licensed professional and check local permits.
  • Edible and toxic plants: never eat a plant or mushroom based only on an online photo. Check plant toxicity for pets with the ASPCA or your vet.

Calculators and frost dates

Calculator results are estimates from the measurements you enter. Frost dates are 30-year NOAA averages, not forecasts, and a frost after the average date is common. Watch your local forecast before planting tender crops.
