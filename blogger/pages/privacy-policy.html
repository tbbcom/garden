Summary

We collect as little as possible. You can read everything without an account. We receive what you choose to send us (for example, through the contact form), and our service providers, mainly Google, use cookies and similar technologies for hosting, analytics and, if enabled, advertising. You can control or refuse these, as explained below.

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy applies to www.homegardenlot.com and tool.homegardenlot.com (together, "the site"), operated by Home Garden Lot ("we", "us"). It explains what information is collected when you visit, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form messages: your name, email address and message, sent to us by email through Google's Blogger contact form. We use them only to reply and to fix any issue you report.
  • Comments (if enabled on a post): the name, profile and comment you submit through Blogger, which are public.

Information collected automatically

  • Server and log data collected by our hosting providers (Google Blogger for the main site, GitHub Pages for the tool site): IP address, browser type, referring page, pages visited and time of visit. This is used for security and to keep the site running.
  • Cookies and similar technologies placed by us and third parties, described below.

Information that stays on your device

Our calculators and the frost-date and planting-calendar tools run in your browser. ZIP codes and measurements you enter are not sent to us. Your browser downloads a small data file for your ZIP code's first three digits, and may remember your last ZIP code and settings in local storage on your own device so the tool is ready next time. You can clear this anytime in your browser settings.

How we use information

  • To run, secure and improve the site and our tools;
  • To understand which guides are useful, using aggregated analytics;
  • To reply to your messages and correct reported errors;
  • To show advertising that helps keep the site free (if enabled);
  • To comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not build profiles about you ourselves.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. The site may use:

  • Essential cookies set by Blogger and GitHub Pages to deliver pages securely;
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics; on the tool site it is loaded through Google Tag Manager) that count visits and show how pages are used;
  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense), if advertising is enabled, described in the next section.

Where advertising or analytics cookies are in use, visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before they are set, through a consent message provided by Google, and Google Consent Mode keeps these tags in a cookieless, non-identifying mode until you agree. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy link or icon shown on the site, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies won't stop the site or our tools from working.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may use Google AdSense to show ads. When we do:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info (U.S.) or www.youronlinechoices.eu (Europe).
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required, ads are non-personalized until you give consent. Other ad partners, if we add any, will be listed here first.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (loaded through Google Tag Manager on the tool site) to see aggregated information such as which pages are read, how visitors find us and which device types they use. We do not use it to identify you. Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Third-party services

  • Google Blogger: hosting of the main site, comments and the contact form (Google Privacy Policy).
  • GitHub Pages and jsDelivr: hosting of the tool site and its data files (GitHub Privacy Statement, jsDelivr Privacy Policy).
  • Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google AdSense, as described above (Google business data responsibility).
  • Embedded content, such as YouTube videos or Pinterest buttons, behaves as if you visited those sites and is governed by their privacy policies.
  • Affiliate links: if you click a retailer link and buy something, the retailer may set a cookie to credit us. See our Disclaimer & Affiliate Disclosure.

How long we keep information

Contact form emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and for up to 24 months afterwards, then deleted. Analytics data is retained as described above. Public comments stay until you or we remove them.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. This includes rights under the EU and UK GDPR and U.S. state laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar laws in Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and other states.

  • To make a request, contact us. We'll respond within 30 days (45 days where U.S. state law allows) and won't discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
  • "Sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising: we don't sell personal information for money. Advertising cookies may count as "sharing" under some U.S. state laws; you can opt out through the privacy choices shown on the site, Google Ads Settings, or by enabling the Global Privacy Control signal in your browser.
  • If you're in the EEA or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal bases (GDPR): consent for non-essential cookies, analytics and personalized ads; legitimate interests for site security and answering messages.

Children's privacy

The site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK). We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security and international transfers

Both sites are served over HTTPS. Our providers (Google, GitHub) may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we collect so little that there's little to expose.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: use our contact form and mention "Privacy" in your message.
