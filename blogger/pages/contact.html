What to contact us about

  • A mistake in a guide or calculator. Tell us the page and what looks wrong. We check every report against the original source and correct confirmed errors, with a note on the page (see our corrections policy).
  • Gardening questions. We can't diagnose plants from a photo, but we answer general questions and often turn them into new guides.
  • Using our data. Journalists, teachers and bloggers are welcome to use our free frost-date dataset or embed the frost-date tool. Ask us if you need a custom summary.
  • Privacy requests. To access or delete information you've sent us, say so in your message. See our Privacy Policy.
Before you write: for plant problems that need a diagnosis, your local cooperative extension office offers free, local advice, and many run Master Gardener help lines.

What we don't accept

We don't sell links, publish paid guest posts or accept payment to review products. Messages offering these won't get a reply. Business partnership ideas that would genuinely help readers are welcome.
