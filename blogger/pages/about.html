In short

Home Garden Lot publishes practical, tested guides and free calculators for growing food, caring for lawns and plants, and improving your home on a budget. Every guide is checked against university extension research and public data from agencies such as NOAA and the USDA, and every calculator shows its formula.

What you'll find here

  • How-to guides for vegetables, lawns, soil, compost, houseplants and simple home projects, written for U.S. homeowners with metric conversions for readers everywhere.
  • Free garden calculators for mulch, soil, topsoil and gravel, lawn fertilizer, pavers and more, so you buy the right amount the first time.
  • Data tools at tools.homegardenlot.com, including frost dates by ZIP code from 7,247 NOAA weather stations and a planting calendar by ZIP code.

How we make our guides

  1. Start from the best sources. Planting dates, fertilizer rates, soil and pest advice come from land-grant university extension services (such as Penn State, University of Minnesota, Texas A&M and UC Agriculture and Natural Resources), the USDA, NOAA and the EPA. Each guide lists its sources at the end.
  2. Check every number. Quantities, conversions and costs are calculated, not guessed. Our calculators use standard volume formulas and show how they work.
  3. Add real-world experience. Where we've done the job ourselves, we share what happened, including mistakes. Where we haven't, we don't pretend to.
  4. Keep it current. Guides show a "Last reviewed" date and are updated before each growing season.

The full process, including how we use AI writing tools and how we correct errors, is in our Editorial Policy.
