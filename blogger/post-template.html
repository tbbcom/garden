Quick answer

A 4×8 ft raised bed filled 12 inches deep needs 32 cubic feet (1.2 cubic yards) of soil — about 16 bags of 2 cu ft mix. Blend 60% topsoil, 30% compost and 10% aeration (perlite or pine fines), and buy 10% extra for settling.

FAQ

Can I use bagged potting mix to fill a raised bed?

Yes for small beds, but it's 2–3× the cost of a topsoil-compost blend and dries out faster.

How often should I top up raised bed soil?

Add 1–2 inches of compost each spring to replace settling and feed the soil.
