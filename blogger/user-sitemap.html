How to find the right gardening guide in under a minute

Follow these five steps to go from "what's wrong with my plant?" to the exact guide you need.

  1. Pick a topic

    Tap a topic chip above the results, such as raised beds, soil, or pests. The number beside each chip shows how many guides it holds.

  2. Search by plant or problem

    Type one or two keywords into the search bar, like "tomato blight" or "raised bed soil". Every word must match, so add words to narrow the list.

  3. Sort the results

    Choose Newest first for the latest advice, Oldest first for the classics, or A to Z to scan alphabetically.

  4. Open a guide

    Select any card to read it. Cards marked New were published in the last 30 days.

  5. Save or share your filtered list

    The page address updates as you filter. Copy it to bookmark or send the exact list you built, or hit Surprise me for a random guide.

Sitemap FAQ

What is the Home Garden Lot sitemap?

It is a single page that lists every gardening guide published on Home Garden Lot, grouped by topic and searchable. It is built for readers; search engines use our separate XML sitemap.

How often is the guide list updated?

Automatically. The list is read live from our blog feed each time the page loads, so a new guide shows up here as soon as it is published.

How do I find a guide about a specific plant or problem?

Type the plant or problem into the search bar, or choose a topic chip. You can do both at once, and the results narrow as you type.

Is there an XML sitemap for search engines?

Yes. Our XML sitemap is at homegardenlot.com/sitemap.xml. This page is the readers' version of the same index.

Can I share a filtered list with someone?

Yes. The page address updates with your topic, search, sort and page number. Copy the link from your browser and anyone who opens it sees the same list.

A link is broken, or I want a guide on a new topic. What should I do?

Send us a note on the contact page. Corrections are reviewed by the editorial team and fixed as quickly as we can.

Does the sitemap work without JavaScript?

The interactive list needs JavaScript. Without it, you can browse everything from the XML sitemap or the home page.
